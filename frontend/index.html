How UpToSkill works

Peer learning in three steps. No long courses, just the help you need, when you need it.

1

Ask or book

Post a doubt, ask for Instant Help or book a mentor by skill, language and time.

2

Learn live

Join a live room with video, chat and a shared code editor. AI writes the session notes.

3

Rate and grow

Rate your mentor, keep your streak and earn badges. Mentors earn credits and level up.
